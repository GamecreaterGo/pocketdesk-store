Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Who We Are

PocketDesk ("we", "us", "our") operates this website and sells productivity hardware directly to consumers. We are based in Nairobi, Kenya. Our contact email is businessgoalmust@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address — to fulfil your order.
  • Payment details — processed securely by our payment provider. We do not store card numbers.
  • Messages you send us via the contact form — to respond to your enquiry.

We do not collect any data through tracking cookies, analytics tools, or third-party advertising platforms at this time. If this changes, this policy will be updated before any such tools are activated.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil your order, including shipping and handling returns.
  • To respond to your customer support requests.
  • To send you order confirmation and shipping notifications by email.
  • To comply with legal obligations (e.g. tax records).

We do not use your personal data for advertising profiling or sell it to third parties.

4. Who We Share Your Data With

We share your data only with service providers who help us operate our business:

  • Shipping carriers — your name and address are passed to the carrier to deliver your order.
  • Payment processors — your payment details are handled by our payment provider under their own privacy policy.
  • Hosting providers — our website and order data are stored on servers operated by our hosting provider.

All third parties are contractually required to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain order records for up to 7 years to comply with tax and accounting obligations. Contact form messages are kept for up to 2 years. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time (see Section 7).

6. Data Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Payment processing is handled by a PCI-compliant payment provider. We do not store payment card details on our servers.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at businessgoalmust@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

This website uses only essential browser storage (localStorage) to maintain your shopping cart during a session. No tracking cookies or third-party cookies are currently set.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, please contact us at businessgoalmust@gmail.com or via the contact page.
