Refund & Returns Policy

We stand behind our products. Here is exactly how returns and refunds work.

Last updated: August 13, 2026

↩ 30-day returns · 2-year warranty · Contact us at businessgoalmust@gmail.com to start a return.

1. Dispatch & Delivery

Orders are dispatched within up to 14 calendar days (2 weeks) of confirmed payment. Delivery times vary by destination and are provided as estimates only. You will receive a shipping notification by email once your order has been dispatched.

2. Returns

You may return any item within 30 days of the delivery date, provided the item is in its original condition and packaging. We will issue a full refund to your original payment method once the returned item is received and inspected. Return shipping costs for change-of-mind returns are the responsibility of the customer.

To initiate a return, email businessgoalmust@gmail.com with your order number and reason for return. We will respond within one business day with return instructions.

Return shipping costs: If you are returning an item because it is defective, damaged on arrival, or incorrect, PocketDesk will cover all return shipping costs. For all other returns (e.g. change of mind), the customer is responsible for return shipping costs.

3. Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we will process your refund within 5–10 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout.

You will receive an email confirmation when your refund has been processed. The time for the funds to appear in your account depends on your bank or card provider.

4. Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged or defective, please contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the damage. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you.

5. Wrong Item Received

If you receive an incorrect item, contact us immediately. We will ship the correct item and cover all return costs for the incorrect item.

6. Non-Returnable Items

Items are not eligible for return if:

  • More than 30 days have passed since delivery.
  • The item shows signs of damage caused by misuse, liquid exposure, or unauthorised modification.

7. Warranty

All PocketDesk products carry a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use. This warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or damage from unauthorised modifications.

To make a warranty claim, email businessgoalmust@gmail.com with your order number and a description of the issue. Warranty replacements are sent at no charge.

8. Contact

All return and refund requests must be initiated by contacting us at businessgoalmust@gmail.com. We aim to respond to all enquiries within one business day, Monday to Friday.
